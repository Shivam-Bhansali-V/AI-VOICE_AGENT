How It Works

A full sales call, fully automated — from dial to log.

1

Call Triggered

Vapi places an outbound call to the lead using your configured AI agent.

2

Agent Introduces Itself

The agent opens the conversation, adapts to the lead's language, and builds rapport.

3

Sales Conversation

Handles objections, answers questions, qualifies the lead, and pushes toward a demo booking.

4

Logged to Sheets

Transcript, summary, outcome, duration, and cost — all pushed to Google Sheets in real time.
