How it works

1. Vapi places the call using a configured AI agent.

2. At call end, Vapi posts an end-of-call-report to our webhook.

3. The server extracts caller, duration, outcome, cost, and Vapi's own summary.

4. A new row is appended to your Google Sheet — ready to view on the dashboard.
